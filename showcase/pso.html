Program Specific
Outcomes

Specific competencies that students are expected to possess upon graduation from the CSE programme.

The Program Specific Outcomes of the B.E. Computer Science and Engineering programme define the specific technical and professional skills that graduates will have acquired. These outcomes are tailored to the unique strengths of the CSE programme and complement the broader Programme Outcomes defined by the NBA framework.

Outcomes
PSO 1

Design and develop efficient software solutions using modern programming paradigms, frameworks, and software engineering principles.

PSO 2

Apply Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science techniques to analyse data and build intelligent real-world applications.

PSO 3

Build secure, scalable, and efficient networked systems with an understanding of cybersecurity principles and cloud computing.

PSO 4

Demonstrate effective project management, teamwork, and leadership skills in the development and deployment of software systems.
