Introduction

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Arunachala College of Engineering for Women was established in 2010 and is approved by AICTE and affiliated to Anna University. The department is committed to producing technically competent, ethically grounded, and innovation-driven engineers who are ready to meet global industry demands.

Objectives
  • Provide strong theoretical and practical foundations in Computer Science.
  • Develop problem-solving and analytical thinking skills in students.
  • Encourage research, innovation, and entrepreneurship.
  • Foster industry–academia collaboration for real-world exposure.
  • Develop ethical, socially responsible computing professionals.

Vision
Department of Computer Science and Engineering
"

To be a centre of excellence in Computer Science and Engineering education, producing globally competent professionals who contribute to technological advancement and societal development.

What Our Vision Means
  • Centre of Excellence — Maintaining the highest academic and research standards in CSE education.
  • Global Competence — Preparing students to compete and contribute at an international level.
  • Technological Advancement — Driving innovation through cutting-edge research and development.
  • Societal Development — Using technology to solve real-world problems and improve lives.
Our Path Forward

We envision a department where every student graduates not just with technical knowledge, but with the creativity, ethics, and leadership skills to make a meaningful impact. Through continuous curriculum innovation, industry partnerships, and research initiatives, we strive to remain at the forefront of computing education.

Our Mission Statements
M1
Quality Education
Provide quality education with strong theoretical and practical foundations that prepare students for real-world challenges in computing and technology.
M2
Research & Innovation
Encourage research, innovation, and entrepreneurship among students and faculty through dedicated labs, funding, and mentorship programs.
M3
Industry–Academia Collaboration
Foster strong partnerships with industry leaders to provide students with internships, live projects, and exposure to current technologies and practices.
M4
Ethical Professionals
Develop ethical, socially responsible computing professionals who understand the impact of technology on society and act with integrity.
M5
Lifelong Learning
Instil a culture of continuous learning and adaptability so graduates remain relevant in the ever-evolving field of computer science.
